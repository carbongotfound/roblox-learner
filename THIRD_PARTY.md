# Open Pixel2Play inference adapter

`src/roblox_learner/p2p.py` adapts architecture, masks and action mappings from [elefant-ai/open-p2p](https://github.com/elefant-ai/open-p2p), revision `a329d98cbe62119679a254d71bea6446773541bc`. The original model weights are downloaded separately from the upstream-linked [guaguaa/open-p2p](https://huggingface.co/guaguaa/open-p2p). No weights are redistributed in this repository.
